Hello! I am Sarthak, a Master’s degree graduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA. I completed my undergraduate studies in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras.

My research interests broadly lie in the fields of Computer Vision and Generative AI. I have worked on topics such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Diffusion Models, 3D Vision, Semantic Segmentation, Object Detection, and Scene Understanding. More recently, I have been exploring emerging areas such as multi-modal learning, vision-language models, and autonomous systems.


Previously, I interned at Samsung Research America (SRA) as a Computer Vision Intern, where I focused on optimizing mobile-efficient vision transformers and deploying lightweight models for edge devices.

I pursed research at the Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc Bangalore, contributing to projects in Generative AI and 3D Scene Understanding. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with [Prof. Kaushik Mitra](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/kmitra/) on Unsupervised Depth Estimation and had the opportunity to collaborate with [Prof. Mohit Gupta](https://wisionlab.com/people/mohit-gupta/) to work on Single Photon Cameras (SPC) for low-light depth estimation.

I thrive in the intersection of innovation and practical application. I am passionate about building scalable, real-world AI systems at the intersection of vision, learning, and efficiency.
